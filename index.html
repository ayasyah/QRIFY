Frequently asked questions

Is QRIFY free to use?

QRIFY's basic QR generation features are free to use. Any future paid features will be clearly identified before purchase.

Does QRIFY upload my QR content?

The QR content and optional logo are processed in your browser by the QR library. The website hosting provider may still receive ordinary technical request data when you visit the site.

Will my QR code expire?

QRIFY creates a standard static QR code. It does not expire by itself, but a website link inside it may stop working if that destination changes or is removed.

Why should I test my QR code?

Testing helps confirm that the code scans correctly and leads to the intended destination. Strong contrast and a clear quiet margin help scanning.
