About QRIFY

QRIFY is a simple online tool designed to help people create QR codes for everyday sharing. You can generate codes for website links, plain text and WiFi network details, customize the colors, optionally add a logo, and save the result as a PNG image.

Our goal

Our goal is to make QR code creation straightforward and accessible on phones, tablets and computers. We aim to provide clear instructions and a useful experience without requiring an account for basic generation.

How the tool works

QRIFY uses a browser-based QR code library to create the code on your device. The text or network details you enter are encoded into the QR image. Avoid encoding private information unless you understand that anyone who obtains the QR image may be able to read or use its contents.
