QRIFY starter website

FILES
- index.html: homepage and QR generator UI
- style.css: responsive dark navy/electric blue design
- script.js: URL, text, WiFi QR generation, logo upload and PNG download
- guides.html: original help content
- about.html, contact.html, privacy.html, terms.html: information pages
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml: replace YOUR-DOMAIN.example after buying/connecting your domain
- assets/favicon.svg: site icon

RUN LOCALLY
1. Open this folder in Visual Studio Code.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or use the VS Code Live Server extension.
3. Internet access is required to load the QRCodeStyling library from its CDN.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com in contact.html with an email address you control.
2. Replace every YOUR-DOMAIN.example in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your real domain.
3. Review and adapt privacy.html and terms.html to your actual hosting, analytics, advertising, cookie and legal practices.
4. Test all QR types, logo uploads, PNG download and mobile layout.
5. Do not add AdSense ad code until the site is approved. Do not create ads.txt until Google provides your publisher details.
6. The QR library is loaded from jsDelivr CDN. For production, review its licensing and availability and consider hosting a pinned copy locally.

NOTE
This is a starter project, not a guarantee of Google indexing, AdSense approval or income. A site needs useful original content, good navigation, policy compliance and real visitors.
